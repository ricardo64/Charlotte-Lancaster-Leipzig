Question 1
A financial auditing firm finds that 5% of companies commit fraud. An automated screening algorithm flags 90% of fraudulent companies, but also incorrectly flags 4% of legitimate companies. If a company is flagged by the algorithm, what is the probability that it actually committed fraud?

Explanation:

Use Bayes' Theorem: P(Fraud | Flagged) = P(Flagged | Fraud) × P(Fraud) / P(Flagged).
• P(Fraud) = 0.05, P(Legitimate) = 0.95
• P(Flagged | Fraud) = 0.90 → P(Fraud ∩ Flagged) = 0.05 × 0.90 = 0.045
• P(Flagged | Legitimate) = 0.04 → P(Legitimate ∩ Flagged) = 0.95 × 0.04 = 0.038
• Total P(Flagged) = 0.045 + 0.038 = 0.083
• P(Fraud | Flagged) = 0.045 / 0.083 ≈ 0.5422 (or 54.22%).
Question 2
A discrete random variable X takes values x = {1, 2, 4, 5} with probabilities 0.20, 0.30, 0.40, and 0.10 respectively. What is the standard deviation, SD(X)?

Explanation:

1. Expected value E(X) = (1×0.20) + (2×0.30) + (4×0.40) + (5×0.10) = 0.2 + 0.6 + 1.6 + 0.5 = 2.9.
2. Variance Var(X) = Σ (x - 2.9)² × P(x):
  • (1 - 2.9)² × 0.20 = 3.61 × 0.20 = 0.722
  • (2 - 2.9)² × 0.30 = 0.81 × 0.30 = 0.243
  • (4 - 2.9)² × 0.40 = 1.21 × 0.40 = 0.484
  • (5 - 2.9)² × 0.10 = 4.41 × 0.10 = 0.441
  • Total Var(X) = 0.722 + 0.243 + 0.484 + 0.441 = 1.53.
3. SD(X) = √1.53 ≈ 1.2369.
Question 3
A consulting firm submits 10 independent project bids. The probability of winning any individual bid is 0.25. What is the probability that the firm wins at least 2 bids?

Explanation:

Use the Complement Rule: P(X ≥ 2) = 1 - [P(X = 0) + P(X = 1)].
• P(X = 0) = (0.75)¹⁰ ≈ 0.0563
• P(X = 1) = 10 × (0.25)¹ × (0.75)⁹ ≈ 0.1877
• P(X < 2) = 0.0563 + 0.1877 = 0.2440
• P(X ≥ 2) = 1 - 0.2440 = 0.7560.
Question 4
Customer service inquiries arrive at a corporate helpdesk at a Poisson rate of 12 per hour. What is the probability that exactly 3 inquiries arrive during a 15-minute interval?

Explanation:

Convert hourly rate to 15 minutes: λ = 12 × (15/60) = 3 inquiries per 15 minutes.
Using Poisson PMF: P(X = 3) = e⁻³ × (3³ / 3!) = e⁻³ × (27 / 6) = 4.5 × e⁻³.
Since e⁻³ ≈ 0.049787 → P(X = 3) = 4.5 × 0.049787 ≈ 0.2240.
Question 5
Daily sales volume (in thousands of Euros) at a boutique retail shop is uniformly distributed between €10 thousand and €30 thousand. What is the probability that on a given day, sales volume is between €18 thousand and €27 thousand?

Explanation:

For a continuous Uniform distribution U(a, b) with a = 10, b = 30:
P(c ≤ X ≤ d) = (d - c) / (b - a) = (27 - 18) / (30 - 10) = 9 / 20 = 0.4500 (or 45%).
Question 6
A machine fills flour bags with a mean weight of μ = 1000 g and standard deviation σ = 20 g, normally distributed. What proportion of flour bags weigh less than 965 g?

Explanation:

1. Standardize to Z-score: Z = (X - μ) / σ = (965 - 1000) / 20 = -35 / 20 = -1.75.
2. Look up P(Z < -1.75) on Standard Normal table:
  • P(Z < -1.75) = 0.0401 (or 4.01%).
Question 7
Weights of cereal boxes are normally distributed with mean μ = 500 g and standard deviation σ = 10 g. Below what weight do the lightest 5% of cereal boxes lie? (Note: Z for 5th percentile is Z = -1.645).

Explanation:

Rearrange standard Z formula X = μ + (Z × σ):
• X = 500 + (-1.645 × 10) = 500 - 16.45 = 483.55 g.
Question 8
A computer system experiencing technical support tickets follows a Poisson process with an average time between tickets of μ = 10 minutes. What is the probability that the time until the next support ticket arrives is at most 5 minutes?

Explanation:

Time between events follows Exponential Distribution with mean μ = 10.
Cumulative probability P(X ≤ x0) = 1 - e^(-x0 / μ):
• P(X ≤ 5) = 1 - e^(-5 / 10) = 1 - e^(-0.5)
• Since e^(-0.5) ≈ 0.60653 → P(X ≤ 5) = 1 - 0.60653 = 0.3935 (or 39.35%).
Question 9
An airline accepts n = 100 reservations for a flight with 90 seats. Each passenger shows up independently with probability p = 0.85. Using the Normal approximation to the Binomial distribution with continuity correction, what is the probability that the flight is overbooked (X ≥ 91 passengers show up)?

Explanation:

1. Binomial parameters: mean μ = np = 100 × 0.85 = 85. Variance σ² = np(1-p) = 100 × 0.85 × 0.15 = 12.75. Standard deviation σ = √12.75 ≈ 3.5707.
2. Continuity Correction for P(X ≥ 91) → evaluate P(X_continuous ≥ 90.5).
3. Standardize Z = (90.5 - 85) / 3.5707 = 5.5 / 3.5707 ≈ 1.54.
4. P(Z ≥ 1.54) = 1 - P(Z ≤ 1.54) = 1 - 0.9382 = 0.0681 (or 6.81%).
Question 10
A study shows that 60% of business administration students take accounting modules, 40% take finance modules, and 25% take both. What is the probability that a randomly chosen student takes accounting OR finance (or both)?

Explanation:

Use the Addition Rule for probabilities: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B).
• P(A ∪ B) = 0.60 + 0.40 - 0.25 = 0.7500 (or 75%).
